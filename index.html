Our story

Founded on the belief that fewer, better things serve you longer.

Maison Verre began in a single atelier room with two tailors and a conviction: that clothing built to be worn for a decade is more luxurious than clothing built to be replaced every season.

Every piece is developed in small batches with mills we've worked with for years, cut to fit the way people actually move, and finished by hand before it ever reaches you.
